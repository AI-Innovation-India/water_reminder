# Turn your image into pixel art

Ways to get a pixel-art buddy for Water Buddy, from easiest to best-looking.

## 1. Use the built-in converter (quickest)

1. Right-click the tray icon, then **Character > Create your own...**
2. Type a name and click **Choose standing...** to pick your image.
3. Leave **Turn my pictures into pixel art** and **Remove the plain background** ticked.
4. Adjust the **Pixel detail** slider. Lower is chunkier and cuter, higher keeps more detail.
5. Optional: leave **Make the drinking and celebrating poses for me** ticked (adds a glass and sparkles), or choose your own drinking and celebrating pictures.
6. Tick **Use it right away** and press **Save buddy**.

To delete a buddy later: Settings and stats > Characters > **Delete** under it (click twice to confirm).

## 2. Free online tools

- **Pixilart** (pixilart.com) and **Piskel** (piskelapp.com): free pixel editors in the browser. Import your picture, then draw over it.
- **Lospec** (lospec.com/pixel-art-converters): a list of free "image to pixel art" converters. Upload a picture and download the result.
- **Photopea** (photopea.com): a free Photoshop-like editor. Use Image > Image Size to shrink the picture to about 48 px tall, then Filter > Pixelate.

## 3. Desktop apps for the best quality

- **Aseprite** (paid, about $20): the standard pixel-art tool, best for hand-cleaning faces.
- **LibreSprite**: a free version of Aseprite.
- **GIMP** (free): shrink with Image > Scale Image, using no interpolation.

## 4. AI image generators

Try a prompt such as:

> pixel art character, 48 pixels tall, transparent background, front view, chibi style

Ask for the standing, drinking and celebrating poses separately.

## Tips for a good result

- Use a picture of one subject on a plain background. Full-body or half-body pictures work better than group shots.
- Aim for about 32 to 64 px tall. Smaller looks cuter, and the app scales it up by whole numbers so it stays crisp.
- Save as a PNG with a transparent background.
- Keep the same size for all three poses.
- Real faces lose a lot of detail at this size. A simple, high-contrast picture gives the best result.

## Using a finished PNG from another tool

1. Right-click the tray icon > **Character > Create your own...**
2. Untick **Turn my pictures into pixel art**.
3. Choose your PNGs: **standing** is required, **drinking** and **celebrating** are optional.
4. Press **Save buddy**.

Or copy a folder into your characters folder (tray > Character > **Add your own...**) with `standing.png`, `drinking.png`, optional `celebrate.png` and optional `character.json` (`{"name": "Robin"}`), then tray > Character > **Reload characters**. Each file must be under 2 MB.

## Rights

Use your own pictures, or ones you have the right to use. Pictures of real people and characters from films or shows belong to their owners: keep those buddies for your own use and do not publish them.
